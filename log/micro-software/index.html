A place to practice.

A violin practice app that keeps track of the key, the exercise, and what needs work tomorrow. I built it for a music stand: one task on screen, with the sound controls close to hand.

Try the modal exercise. Start a drone, then play the notes against it. The highlighted note gives each mode its character.

Inside the drone

Each voice adds eight sine waves at whole-number multiples of its pitch. Four voices sit at the tonic, a 3:2 fifth, and two voices at the octave. Their envelopes rise in turn, giving a continuous tuning reference a gentle pulse. The note buttons use equal temperament, so you can hear the difference against the drone’s pure fifth.

This excerpt uses the app’s mode intervals and additive drone recipe. In daily use, the full app rotates keys over 24 weeks, moves through scales, Bach and improvisation, and stores sessions and recordings on the device.

Why show the evidence?

A description can omit an amenity. “Not mentioned” means the text supplied no match; it does not establish that the apartment lacks it. This scorer is a way to order a reading list. The description remains available for judgment.

An editor for a gallery.

Incubator’s staff needed to publish exhibitions without managing a repository. I built a small editing interface over the gallery’s structured content, with image uploads and dates that determine where each show appears.

Edit the title or dates, then apply the changes. Move the preview date to see the exhibition change between upcoming, current, and past.

How publishing works in the full site
  1. The editor prepares text, dates, and resized images.
  2. A Cloudflare Worker checks staff authorization.
  3. The worker commits the content to the gallery’s repository.
  4. The site rebuilds from that content.
