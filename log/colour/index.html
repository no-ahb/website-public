The ten-minute excerpt starts at 7:00 in the 12–13 November 2025 recording, matching the section selected in the filming controller. It uses the original normalized data and a 400 mm motion scale around a 500 mm centre. Browser playback repeats the excerpt; the filming controller's final fade-out is omitted.

The ocean surface is illustrative. Carriages show target positions, without the hardware's smoothing or motor dynamics. Sound is synthesized from string crossings. In pointer mode, gestures send waves from the edges toward the centre. The surface produces heave as crests pass under the buoy; slopes drive its surge and sway response. The buoy stays centred and tilts to make lateral movement visible. This is an illustrative response, not a reconstruction of measured orientation.
