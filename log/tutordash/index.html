I've tutored SAT and ACT for a couple of years. The teaching was fun; the admin was not: scheduling lessons, sending homework, grading by hand, and writing a recap after every class.

I started with an autograder. It grew into a platform that keeps a running account of the student, the tutor's work, and the business around it. Tutors and agencies now use it with their own students.

Current product components and focused simulations. All people and data are fictional.

The tutor dashboard

The next lesson sits beside the work that leads into it. As students work, the tutor can see what is ready, what is unfinished, and what needs attention.
